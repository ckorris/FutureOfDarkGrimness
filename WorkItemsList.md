## Movement

- [~] 159 — **(Renumbered from #154, reconciliation 9 — master's #154 is destroyed-transport morale.)** Intermittent `DefinePathStage` cohesion crash from AI/auto movement. **Root cause found + FIXED 2026-07-03 (25% -> 0% repro on the HEF smoke); awaiting GUI hand-verification.** The crash (`Breaks cohesion: Model is further than 1 inch from the closest model`) reproduced at **5/20** with a High Elf Fleets smoke army — but, contrary to the "mixed base sizes" hypothesis, **every** hit was in **`ConsolidateStage`**, not the normal movement path. **Mechanism:** after a melee a unit that lost a *middle* model has survivors >1" apart (a casualty hole); the consolidation resolvers only offered a **rigid unit-wide delta** (or an EOF hold), which can't re-form the hole — and with a 1" Disengage cap often *can't* be fixed at all — yet `ConsolidateStage` enforced coherency strictly, so no legal consolidation existed -> throw. **Fix (with the user):** (1) the consolidation resolvers (`AiConsolidationMoveResolver` engine + `ConsolidationMoveResolver` app) now **re-form** the survivors toward their centroid within the cap (`CohesiveFormation.ReformTowardWithinCap`/`IsCohesive`) instead of a rigid delta when the unit is broken — the "attempt to bring them together"; (2) `ConsolidateStage` coherency is **lenient** (`MovementUtilities.ValidateConsolidationPaths`/`ValidateCoherencyNotWorsened`) — a consolidation is only rejected for coherency if it makes coherency *worse* than before the move (mirrors the enemy-standoff "only penalise moves that close the distance"), so a hold is always legal (no trap) but the unit can't scatter; cap/terrain/enemy checks stay strict. **Enemy-crossing variant (AH) = a separate geometry bug, also fixed:** `BaseShapeGeometry.SurfaceGap2D` (SAT) reported a **positive (clear) gap** for a circle whose centre is **inside** a rectangle (a zero-width projection read `overlap == 0` on every axis as "separated"), so the move-through pass-through guard false-positived "Moves through an enemy unit" on a stationary model a melee pile-in had stacked concentric inside a large base. Fixed `overlap <= 0f` -> `< 0f` (touching stays gap 0, circles byte-unchanged; only containment flips to overlapping). **Also fixed a latent mixed-base bug** (the old hypothesis, a third code path): `CohesiveFormation.PackGrid` spaced the whole grid by the largest base, stranding small models >1" from neighbours — rewrote it to pack per-row edge-to-edge at each model's own size and never leave a row with a single neighbourless model. **And the deeper cause fixed 2026-07-04** (the melee stacking itself): the overlap was traced (before/after gap snapshots) to **`PileInStage`** — a defender piling toward its charger only obstacle-checked the charging unit, so it plowed straight through a **third-party enemy** unit's base (overlaps to -3.5, compounding). Fixed by clamping the pile-in step against every enemy of the defender except the charging unit (new `GetEnemyModelFootprints(..., excludeUnit)` overload) + a facing-aware contact cap (`NearestB2BAt`). Four independent root causes, one symptom. Engine 1134/0, app 78/0; **HEF/AH/TitanLords/Saurian/Ratmen/Robot: 0/24 stacking, 0/24 crashes** (was HEF crash 5/20, AH crash ~1/13, AH stacking 19/20); default smoke exit 0. Filed 2026-07-02 from the #156 hero-join smoke-flake investigation. ([WorkItems/159](WorkItems/159-definepath-cohesion-crash.md))
