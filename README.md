## A New Way to Play GrimDark Future
Video game version of GDF. NOT a Tabletop simulator.
Play networked multiplayer, hotseat multiplayer, solo against a competent bot, or mix and match.
Imports armies straight from Army Forge.
Supports all GDF armies and all their special rules.
Great for playing online, trying new armies, or getting a game done in 20 minutes.


## More than TTS
Powered by a complete rules engine that steps each player through each phase and every action of the game, from placing terrain to activating obscure special rules.
You don’t even need to know the rules.


## TacticianBot gives you a solo challenge
FDG comes with a bot that weighs the situation on the table and makes calculated decisions.
Designed like more classic strategy game AIs using heuristics.
Can put bots on your own team, or team up against them with your friends.
Also includes DerpBot, which emulates the solo AI rules from OnePageRules, for beginners.

## Play with your Friends
Host a game, and let your friends join via the game list or direct connection.
Play 1v1, on teams, or a chaotic free-for-all.

## Other features:
Option to randomize terrain/objective placement to get into the fight faster.
“Probabilistic” mode that uses the most likely outcome in every calculation. 5 attacks and Quality 4 will always deal 2.5 wounds. A few exceptions made for binary outcomes like morale tests.
UI that previews what you can hit (and what can hit you) before every move.
Save and load games, including multiplayer.

## What it is not:
A total replacement for the tabletop experience. No software should ever be. The best way to play will always be in-person with friends using hand-painted physical models.
Officially affiliated with OnePageRules.
3D, though the rules engine is close to supporting it.

## Bugs
Use the bug reporter in the escape menu, which also sends a save (if you’re the host) and chat logs.
Can also tell me on Discord or open an issue.
