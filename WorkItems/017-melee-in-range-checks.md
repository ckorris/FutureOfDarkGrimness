# 017 — In-range attacker/defender determination (melee)

**Status**: done — reopened 2026-06-14 for an incomplete empty-pool guard; follow-up fix merged to origin/master 2026-06-14 (engine `2bb10eb`)
**Related**: engine `3b12c61` (rebased onto #011/#089); #011/#089 (AI charge-to-contact + enemy-move validation — complementary), #018 (pile-in, feeds positions), #020 (fatigue), #022 (vertical melee range), #051 (furious charge gate)

## Goal
Replace the "everyone fights" stubs in `DetermineInRangeAttackersStage` and `DetermineInRangeDefendersStage` with real range gating. After a charge + pile-in, only attacking models within melee range of an enemy model may strike, and only defending models within melee range of an attacker may strike back. Range is `MELEE_RANGE_INCHES_HORIZONTAL` (2") base-to-base in the horizontal plane, OR within `MELEE_RANGE_INCHES_VERTICAL` (4") accounting for elevation (GF v3.5.1). "Done" = a model out of range contributes none of its weapons to the swing/strike-back pool, and an integration test pins the behavior (in-range model fights; out-of-range model in the same unit does not).

## Decisions
- **Gate the empty-pool guard on `AvailableWeapons.Count`, not `inRange.Count`** (2026-06-14, reopen): the swing pool — not the in-range model count — is the precondition `ChooseMeleeWeaponStage` actually enforces. In-range models can carry no melee weapon (ranged-only survivors), so a model-count guard leaks. Checking the rebuilt pool covers both "nobody in range" and "in-range but melee-weaponless" with one condition, and stays in lockstep with whatever `SetInRangeAttackers` produces.
- **Integration via context, not per-swing filtering** (signed off 2026-06-14): the determine stages compute in-range model sets and the context rebuilds the weapon pool from them, rather than filtering weapons per swing. Range is computed once (post-pile-in), the seam is clean, and weapons aren't owner-tagged so per-swing filtering would have needed a per-model map anyway.
- **Vertical handled now** (signed off 2026-06-14): a model is in range if ≤2" horizontal b2b AND ≤4" vertical. Both constants already existed; cheap to include. #022 still owns the separate `ChooseMeleeDefenderStage` vertical-selection TODO.
- **Dead-model fix went in the shared util (Option 1), not just the melee gates** (signed off 2026-06-14): `MinDistanceBetweenUnits` skipping dead models is the correct definition of unit-to-unit distance and fixes the same latent corpse-distance bug in shooting (`RollToHitStage` / `DetermineHitRollNeededStage` range bands) at one site with no duplication. Deliberate, recorded scope expansion beyond pure melee — full suite re-run confirmed no shooting regression.
- **Guard routes through consolidate, not straight to finish**: mirrors the existing `OnDefenderKilled` path so a charger that wipes its target via Impact still consolidates.
- **Surfaced cause of the empty-pool crash**: Impact hits can destroy the defending unit *during the charge*, before the swing — a legitimate, common case, not just the corpse-charge edge. This is why the guard is load-bearing, not merely defensive.

## Outcome
Replaced the "everyone fights" stubs with real melee range gating: only models within 2" horizontal / 4" vertical of a living enemy may strike or strike back, and only their weapons enter the swing pool (via `MeleeRangeUtilities` + in-range sets on `CombatActionContext`, consumed by the two determine stages, `StrikeBackStage`, and `OfferStrikeBackStage`). Fixed the root-cause corpse-distance bug in the shared `MinDistanceBetweenUnits` (now live-models-only — also corrects shooting range bands) and added an empty-in-range-set guard that resolves a melee with no attacks (defender wiped by Impact, or any future reach gap) via the consolidate path instead of crashing `ChooseMeleeWeaponStage`. Engine suite 487/0; headless smoke clean. Deferred: consolidation nuance is inherited from the existing path; no new deferrals. Possible follow-up not pursued: charge legality vs. live reach is now consistent, so no separate shooting-bug ticket was needed (folded in here).
